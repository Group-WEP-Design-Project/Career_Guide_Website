CV Preparation

Learn how to write a clear and compelling CV for internship applications.

A strong CV should include:

  • A short profile focused on your target role.
  • Relevant projects, skills, education, and achievements.
  • Clear action words and consistent formatting.
🎤

Interview Tips

Prepare for common questions and build confidence before your interview day.

Before your interview:

  • Research the company and understand the role.
  • Prepare concise examples using the STAR method.
  • Practise your introduction and prepare questions.
💡

Career Advice

Get practical guidance for choosing the right path and making smart decisions.

Make better career decisions:

  • Compare your interests with the work each path involves.
  • Speak with people who already work in the field.
  • Try projects or internships before committing.
🚀

Skill Development

Explore practical learning paths to strengthen your technical and soft skills.

Build skills with purpose:

  • Choose one technical skill and practise it weekly.
  • Build small projects that demonstrate your progress.
  • Strengthen communication, teamwork, and adaptability.
